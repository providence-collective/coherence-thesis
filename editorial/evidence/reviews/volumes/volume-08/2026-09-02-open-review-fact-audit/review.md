## Status model

- **Verified:** the retained wording is supported at the stated scope by the attached primary or authoritative source.
- **Provisional:** the attached evidence supports only a bounded or unsettled statement, and the uncertainty is stated in the claim map.
- **Withdrawn:** the earlier claim is not retained because its source, wording, precision, or inference did not survive review.

`Verified` describes the evidence attachment and bounded wording. It does not mean universal consensus or professional endorsement. `Provisional` is a publication status, not a request that a reader accept the claim on trust.
