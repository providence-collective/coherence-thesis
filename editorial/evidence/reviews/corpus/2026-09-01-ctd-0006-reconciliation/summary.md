## Canon result

Human judgment may consider demonstrated relational coherence when choosing or appointing a person to bounded stewardship. A participant may voluntarily offer scoped, role-relevant, privacy-preserving evidence derived from a private longitudinal record containing limited biometrics and consented reflections from others. The underlying record remains participant-held. The evidence is one input, never the decision-maker or sole basis, and never a measure of whether the person's own physiology matches an ideal.

Refusal creates no adverse inference and changes no membership, provision, political voice, vote weight, human worth, or standing. Accessible non-biometric routes must carry equal standing. Contributors retain consent and evidence control. A published rule must establish role relevance, evidence quality, context, validation, measurement-error limits, and stopping conditions before consequential use.

Live sensing in a public governance meeting remains a separate provisional experiment. It requires private, unanimous, meeting-specific consent from everyone present for the sensed portion, fails closed when a response would be readily inferable, stops on withdrawal without penalty, produces only ephemeral aggregate non-attributable advisory output, cannot pause or decide automatically, admits a pause request without biometrics, and does not make physiological data public.
