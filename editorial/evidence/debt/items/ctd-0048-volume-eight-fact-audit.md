## Debt

Volume VIII relies on a dense, time-sensitive factual accounting of ecology, public health, war, economics, artificial intelligence, law, climate, genetics, surveillance, fertility, loneliness, and sexual behavior. The edition needs a complete claim-to-source apparatus, visible uncertainty, and a public route through which qualified readers can inspect and challenge the record without cluttering the manuscript or implying professional endorsement.

## Evidence

The volume makes precise claims about bee losses, agricultural value, insect biomass, nutrition and cancer, glyphosate, microplastics, active conflicts, deaths and displacement, inflation, AI labor effects, climate, overdose, suicide, 23andMe, social credit, fertility, sperm counts, government transparency, and intimacy. It also presents claims about seven planetary lights, weekday naming, and direct transformations among solstice, Christmas, Samhain, Halloween, Imbolc, and Groundhog Day without adequate historical boundaries. Several citations are secondary organizations or news summaries, and many entries lack authors, exact titles, dates, links, methods, or direct attachment to the sentence they support.

## Paydown criteria

- C1. Map every factual, empirical, medical, legal, historical, and quotation claim in the exact final manuscript.
- C2. Attach every retained factual claim to a primary source or strongest available authoritative source, recording its location, method, population, geography, date, denominator, limitations, and uncertainty.
- C3. Give every time-sensitive claim an exact as-of date and comparison series.
- C4. Connect every supporting citation to the exact manuscript sentence it supports.
- C5. Mark every mapped claim as verified, provisional, or withdrawn, and record contested findings, projections, causal uncertainty, and unresolved methods without describing them as consensus.
- C6. Publish a working open-review process through which qualified readers can inspect the claim map, identify their scope and qualifications, submit attributable conclusions, and have accepted corrections preserved in later versioned editions. State explicitly that provisional medical and legal material is not professional advice or endorsement.
- C7. Present symbolic analogy as analogy. Every retained historical claim must name the relevant language, region, period, and distinction between documented descent and comparison.
- C8. Remove or correct every contradicted, unsupported, or untraceable claim; refresh the map against the exact final manuscript hash; publish the living report; validate reader, continuity, citation, Updates, and audio parity; and obtain exact-preview approval.

On 2026-09-02 the author approved an open-review edition. The governing distinction is now visible and testable: `verified` means the cited evidence supports the bounded statement; `provisional` means evidence exists but interpretation, method, or professional judgment remains open; `withdrawn` means earlier wording was unsupported, contradicted, untraceable, or retired. Provisional status may not rescue a claim contradicted by its evidence. Qualified review is invited through a public process and will inform later versioned editions; this edition must not be described as medically, legally, or historically endorsed.
