## Debt

Volume VIII relies on a dense, time-sensitive factual accounting of ecology, public health, war, economics, artificial intelligence, law, climate, genetics, surveillance, fertility, loneliness, and sexual behavior. Its roots section is a reading list rather than a complete claim-to-source apparatus, and the manuscript itself says the mid-2026 figures must be refreshed before publication.

## Evidence

The volume makes precise claims about bee losses, agricultural value, insect biomass, nutrition and cancer, glyphosate, microplastics, active conflicts, deaths and displacement, inflation, AI labor effects, climate, overdose, suicide, 23andMe, social credit, fertility, sperm counts, government transparency, and intimacy. It also presents claims about seven planetary lights, weekday naming, and direct transformations among solstice, Christmas, Samhain, Halloween, Imbolc, and Groundhog Day without adequate historical boundaries. Several citations are secondary organizations or news summaries, and many entries lack authors, exact titles, dates, links, methods, or direct attachment to the sentence they support.

## Paydown criteria

- C1. Create a claim-level map for every factual, empirical, historical, legal, medical, and quotation claim in the current manuscript.
- C2. Verify every mapped claim against a primary source or the strongest available authoritative source, recording the source, method, population, geography, date, and material limitations.
- C3. Date-stamp every volatile claim and state the exact as-of date, comparison series, population, and denominator where they affect the claim.
- C4. Attach each supporting citation to the exact manuscript wording it supports rather than relying on a source-family reading list.
- C5. Mark contested findings, projections, disputed causal accounts, and unresolved methods at the claim level without flattening them into consensus.
- C6. Obtain qualified subject review for medical and legal claims and record the reviewer's scope and conclusions.
- C7. Use historical scholarship to separate symbolic analogy from cultural descent and narrow each surviving historical claim by language, region, and period.
- C8. Remove or revise any figure, quotation, or genealogy that does not survive verification, then refresh the complete audit against the exact manuscript candidate immediately before publication.

## Partial paydown

Quick triage on 2026-08-22 confirmed that the current manuscript materially improves source posture without completing factual verification. It date-stamps the volatile 2026 accounting, identifies projections and disputed causal accounts, limits the Krefeld result to its regional evidence, keeps farmer-suicide causation plural, distinguishes glyphosate hazard from exposure risk, marks microplastics methods and clinical meaning as unsettled, and narrows the weekday and festival history by language, region, and period. These changes partially advance C3, C5, and C7.

The settled calibration records for `v08-2-the-accounting`, `v08-3-the-present-tense-a-reckoning-of-the-year-2026`, `v08-saturns-day-a-cosmological-opening-and-a-note-on-the-register`, and `v08-the-roots-of-this-volume` preserve those boundaries and explicitly keep CTD-0048 open. They are editorial evidence, not primary-source verification.

The current production review at `editorial/evidence/reviews/volumes/volume-08/2026-07-09-production-pass/` states that fact checking was not performed. Its sentence ledger identifies 130 factual or empirical baseline claims, all still marked `query`, with no citation attachments. Its reviewed source hash, `43fd9d6647eaf788a07b96523e9f2cc7fc8d35009f8d0791b25d40eb2cba10d1`, does not match the current manuscript hash, `60a9339de6656c68129449af471e06230b5ae7ad599001c7126c97152d79c0c7`, so it cannot verify the exact current source.

C1, C2, C4, C6, C8, and the primary-source portions of C5 and C7 remain unmet. CTD-0106 overlaps with the refresh requirement for dated Volume VIII statistics, but it separately owns the Volume VII forward reference and abbreviation-style obligations and is not a duplicate of this claim-level audit.
