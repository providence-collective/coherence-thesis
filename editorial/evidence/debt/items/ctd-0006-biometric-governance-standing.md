## Debt

Volume III leaves unclear whether a COHERENCE record derived partly from physiological signals can affect governance standing, stewardship authority, chamber eligibility, or decision weight.

## Evidence

The ambiguity sits beside strong promises that Providence is not social credit, does not rank people, and preserves an unconditional floor. Revised Volume III now begins political voice from equal standing, while still allowing participant-controlled evidence to inform bounded stewardship. Volume IV says no sensor decides who may speak, vote, or lead. Volume IX says presencing earns "the standing to lead and to steward." Governance consequences could violate equal standing and the stated invariants even if participation is voluntary.

## Paydown criteria

- C1. Record the author's decision on whether biometric-derived personal records may affect governance standing, stewardship, chamber eligibility, deliberative access, decision weight, or votes.
- C2. State which bounded stewardship decisions may consider non-biometric attestations and require relevance, optional disclosure, contestability, equal non-biometric routes, due process, appeal, anti-discrimination controls, and remedies.
- C3. Define the consent and meeting-continuity rule for any live physiological sensing in public governance meetings, including refusal, abstention, missing consent, late arrival, and withdrawal.
- C4. Define data and authority limits for any meeting experiment, including ephemeral processing, aggregation, non-attribution, exclusion from personal records, human control, and the right to request a pause without biometric evidence.
- C5. Name the plausible benefits and detriments in the literature, preserve the uncertainty of the evidence, and state why the selected path remains provisional.
- C6. Reconcile the corpus ledger, affected manuscripts, voice authorities, and transition study, then complete independent semantic, literary, and slop review.
- C7. Preserve publication safety by identifying every invalidated spoken section and preventing merge until matching immutable audio, exact-candidate preview approval, and the remaining manuscript publication gates pass.

## Partial paydown

- Author decision, 2026-08-20: biometric-derived personal records never affect governance standing, stewardship, chamber eligibility, deliberative access, speaking or agenda rights, decision weight, or votes. Non-biometric attestations may inform appointment, delegation, role scope, review, or removal for bounded stewardship only when the evidence is relevant, optional, contestable, and subject to due process, appeal, anti-discrimination controls and review, correction, and remedy. A person who declines an attestation can establish the same role-relevant qualifications through other evidence on equal terms.
- Author decision, 2026-08-20: a public governance meeting may explore live physiological sensing only when every person present for the sensed portion, including participants and observers, privately and affirmatively consents for that specific meeting. The process reveals only activation or nonactivation and never an individual's response or reason. If a response would be readily inferable, sensing does not activate. Unanimity controls activation of sensing, not whether the meeting proceeds. Refusal, abstention, missing consent, late arrival without consent, or withdrawal leaves sensing off or stops it while the meeting continues without penalty.
- Author decision, 2026-08-20: output must be ephemeral, aggregate, non-attributable, advisory, and excluded from personal records. It cannot automatically pause a meeting or decide an outcome. Any person may request a pause without biometric evidence. Public deliberation does not make physiological data public.
- Author decision, 2026-08-20: this is Option 2 and remains provisional while the project names and studies the benefits and detriments in the literature and seeks a more coherent way. Coherent here names an ethical and institutional design, not a political fact established by a sensor.
- Implementation candidate on `edit/ctd-0006-biometric-standing`: the decision record, corpus ledger, Volumes III, IV, and IX, their voice cards, and the transition study are being reconciled through the editorial review workflow. Independent review, exact-candidate preview approval, matching immutable audio, remaining validation, and structured closure are still open.
